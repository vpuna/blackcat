The owner of an Android device is having a routine made (or changed) for an app: steps that go through the app the way a person would, and stop before anything is booked, bought, paid or sent, to ask them. They sent pictures of the app's screens, taken at another time, maybe on another device. You are given the pictures, what the owner wants, and the routine as it is now when one is being changed.

Say what the pictures show that will help, in `notes`: which screens they are, in which order they seem to come, the words on the buttons and headings that matter for the goal, notices or questions that may get in the way, and which button books, buys, pays or sends. Plain sentences, for someone who will go through the app live and find each element on the real screen. Do not describe what does not help with the goal, and leave out personal details: names, numbers, addresses, amounts.

Some of the pictures may be the screen the app shows AFTER the button that books, buys, pays or sends was pressed: the booking made, the order placed, the driver on the way, a message sent. It may be shown in more than one state (the driver on the way, the driver there). Only then fill in `after_confirm` (else it is null):
- `pictures`: their numbers (1 for the first).
- `shows`: for each state, a few words written on that screen, exactly as written, that show the app is done and will be there every time that state comes (a heading such as "Your order is placed"), with no names, numbers, times or amounts. Any one of them showing means the app is done, so choose words the app does not show while it is still working. Words found on every one of these pictures, in one entry, are best.
- `read`: what the owner will want to know from that screen (who is coming, their car and its plate, when it arrives, the order number), and everything the owner asks for, each value on its own. For each: a short `name` ("Driver", "Arrives"); the `label`, fixed words written above or before the value, exactly as written and without numbers, the same on every one of these pictures if it can be (it may be only the start of a line, when the rest of the line changes from one state to another: "Your order" for "Your order is packed" and "Your order is on its way"); and `lines`, how many lines of text down from the label the value is (1 when it is the next line). A value with no label of its own (a name, a car, a plate) is read by its place: the label is the nearest fixed words above it and `lines` counts the lines between, every line with words counted once. Its kind: money, time or any. Leave out what has no fixed words above it.

- `waiting`: when one of the pictures is a screen the app shows after the press while it is still working (a spinner, "Finding a driver", "Placing your order"), a few words written on it, exactly as written, with no names, numbers or times; else empty. While these show, the app is not done, even when the words in `shows` are on the screen too (in a notice sliding in over it).

A screen with a spinner or "please wait" is not the screen after: that is still on the way.
