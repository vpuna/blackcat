A routine goes through an app on an Android device the way a person would, and stops at a confirmation step, before anything is booked, bought, paid or sent, to ask the owner. The owner wants it changed, and said how in their words. You are given the app, the routine (its goal, its values and its numbered steps, each in plain words and as JSON), the change, and what the owner's pictures of the app show, when they sent any.

Say how the change is made (`how`), and in `summary` say in one or two plain sentences what will change, for the owner to read before it is tried:
- steps: the change is in the steps alone, with nothing new to find in the app: a step left out, steps in another order, a longer wait, a value made optional (with `only_with` / `only_without` on the steps for it), a word of a button the owner gives. Give the whole list of steps as they will be, as JSON text, in `steps`, in the same form as now; and, only when the values change, all of them in `params`, as JSON text. Do not make up targets for elements the routine has never seen: a step that needs a new element is `live`.
- live: new steps that must be found in the app: a new screen to go through, an option to choose, something else to tap. Give `at`, the number of the step after which the new steps go (0: at the very start, after the app opens), `replace`, how many steps after that the new ones take the place of (0 when they are only added), and `goal`: what the new steps must do, in plain words, for someone going through the app live from there. Never at or after the confirmation step: the new steps go before it.
- after_confirm: the change is only about what happens after the confirmation button is pressed: waiting for the app's last screen and reading it (who is coming, when, the order number). Only when one of the pictures is that screen. When the owner asks for that and for other changes too, answer for the other changes (steps or live): the screen after the press is taken from the picture all the same.
- unclear: you cannot tell what the owner means, or the change would need the routine to press what books, buys, pays or sends by itself. Ask, in `question`, one short plain question.

Rules:
- The confirmation step stays the last step, as it is, except for one thing in its `result`: when the owner names words the app shows while it is still working after the press ("Finding your driver"), add them as `"waiting": { "textContains": "…" }` (a list for several), with `how` steps. Nothing in the change may press what books, buys, pays or sends.
- Keep each step's `why`, and give a new step one: a few words on what it is for.
- Change only what the owner asked for.
