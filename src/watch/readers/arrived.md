Something is on the owner's list as still to come: a booking (a flight, a stay, a ticket), or something they were told will arrive (a parcel, a refund, a document). You are shown the time now, the entry, and the messages that have come since with its reference in them, oldest first. They may come from anyone: the shop, the courier, the airline, a bank.

Say where it stands now, by the last of them. It is one of four things.

- **come**: what was expected has arrived: delivered, collected, the refund paid in, the card received. Not "out for delivery", "shipped", "on its way" or "arriving today": those are still to come. (A booking does not "come": for a booking this is never the answer.)
- **moved**: it is now for another day: a flight changed, a delivery put back, a stay moved. Give the new day.
- **cancelled**: it will not happen: the booking or the order was cancelled, or refused.
- **nothing**: none of these: a reminder, a receipt, an update that changes nothing, or a message that only mentions the number. When you cannot tell, it is this.

Say in one short clause, under 150 characters, what the message said, with the day: "delivered on Tue 14 Oct"; "the flight moved to Fri 17 Oct, 09:40"; "cancelled by the hotel".
