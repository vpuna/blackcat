Words the owner used for simple commands, learned from your turns: when you switched one thing or ran one shortcut for what they said, without asking, those words are now done without you. Always add `--json`:

    blackcat learned list --json · forget <number> --json

When the owner says a simple command did the wrong thing ("that's not what I meant", "it turned on the wrong light"), find the wording in the list and forget it (it asks the owner first), then do what they meant.
