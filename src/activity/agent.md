blackcat keeps a record of what happened and when, and you can read it: what you did, what it used, what the owner did directly, what was sent to them, and what went wrong. Use it when the owner asks what you did, why something was slow, what used their plan with the model's provider, whether something happened or was sent, or what was changed. Always add `--json`:

    blackcat activity recent [--kind model|command|job|event|owner|sent] [--category <text>] [--since 2h|24h|7d] [--failed] [-n 30] --json
    blackcat activity usage [--since 7d] [--by category|model|day|kind] --json

- **Kinds.** `model`: a call to the model (a chat turn, or a watch or attachment being read), with its model, tokens, cost and time. `command`: a command you ran, with how it was allowed (`decision`) and how it ended. `job`: scheduled work that did something (runs that found nothing to do are only counted). `event`: something worth noting: what a watch's look came to, a check that failed, blackcat started or stopped, a service that ended by itself or needs the owner, an account that wrote to the bot and is not paired, a plugin that was not loaded. `owner`: what the owner did without you: a button tapped (what it said, and its id), a command typed in the chat (its name and first word), something handled directly; and every change to how blackcat is set up or what you may do (a plugin switched on or off, a machine added, a mode, a standing permission given or removed, the channel, a service started or stopped, a backup made or restored by hand). `sent`: what blackcat sent the owner by itself, named by what it was ("the briefing", "reminder 12", a check's alert, a notice from one of their scripts as `notify: <name>`), and whether it went.
- **"What did you do to answer that?" / "why was that slow?"** → `recent --since 10m`. A chat entry shows `ms` (the whole turn, until the reply), `commands` and `commandMs` (time in commands), `apiMs` (time waiting on the model) and `waitedForYouMs` (time waiting for an approval). Say where the time went in plain words.
- **"What used my plan?" / "how much did the school watch use?"** → `usage`, by `category` (chat, each watch by name, attachments), `model` or `day`. Cost is the list price of the calls: a measure of how much of the plan they used, not money charged. Say that once if you quote dollars. `reusedInput` tokens are the cheap ones (instructions sent before and kept); `freshInput` and `output` are what count most.
- **"What have you run on my server?" / "was anything refused?"** → `recent --kind command`, with `--failed` for what was refused, declined or went wrong. Commands that needed approval, were refused or failed are kept in full; ordinary ones show only what was run, without their arguments.
- **"Did my briefing go out?" / "did you remind me about the bank?" / "what did you send me this morning?"** → `recent --kind sent --since 24h`. It says that it was sent, when, and whether it went; it does not hold the text. For what a reminder or a list entry says, use `remind list --all` or `watch show`.
- **"What changed?" / "who switched that off?" / "what am I allowing you to do, and since when?"** → `recent --kind owner`, with `--category` to narrow it ("standing permission", "plugin", "ssh", "service"). These are the owner's own actions: report them as theirs, not as yours.
- **"Did I mark that done?" / "what did I do earlier?"** → `recent --kind owner --since 24h`. A tap shows the button's words and an id (`"✅ Done" · rm:51:d` is reminder 51 marked done; `ap:` is an answer to an approval; `sc:` a shortcut's action; `wi:` a list entry; `ha:` a device).
- **"Has anyone else tried to use the bot?"** → `recent --category channel`. **"Did anything crash?" / "why did WhatsApp stop?"** → `recent --category service --since 7d`, and `--failed` for everything that went wrong.
- **Figures on those entries.** Every `owner` and `sent` entry has `ms` and `ok`. A tap or a typed command: `ms` is how long what it set off took; `error` says why it failed; `by` is `command` when blackcat handled it and `agent` when it was left to you (then `ms` includes your turn); `waitedS` is how long the message took to reach blackcat after it was written (only when 5 s or more: the delay was before blackcat, usually the connection). A command at the terminal: `ms`, and `exit` when it failed. Something sent: `ms` (the channel taking it), `chars`, `error`, and `lateS` when it went a minute or more after its time. A tap marked `gone` did nothing: what it was about no longer existed. A chat turn (`model`, category `chat`) has `waitedS` too, and `queuedMs` when it waited behind an earlier message of theirs. `bc start`, `stop` and `restart` have `ms`. A service is an `event` in category `service: <id>`: `connected` with `ms` (how long after it was started), `connected again` with `offlineS` (how long it was cut off), and `started after waiting N s` when it could not start at once. A voice note is an `event` in category `voice note`: `audioS` (its length), `workS` and `ms` (time to turn it into words).
- **"Why was my reminder late?" / "why did that button take so long?" / "how long do voice notes take?"** → `recent --kind sent`, `--kind owner` or `--category "voice note"` with a `--since`, and compare `ms`, `lateS` and `waitedS`. A reminder that is late with a small `ms` was sent late (blackcat was not running, or it was quiet hours), not slowly. Say which.
- **"How long does blackcat take to start?" / "how often does WhatsApp drop?"** → `recent --category service --since 7d`: each `connected` has the start-up time, each `connected again` the time cut off.
- The record never holds what was said: no messages, no replies, no search results. Don't present it as if it did. To find a message, use the message commands.
- Entries are kept for a limited time (`blackcat activity settings`); daily totals for a year. Changing what is recorded (`activity setup`) needs the owner's approval, and only the owner can clear it.
