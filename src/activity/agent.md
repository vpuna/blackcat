blackcat keeps a record of what happened and when: what you did, what it used, what the owner did directly, what was sent to them, and what went wrong. Use it when the owner asks what you did, why something was slow, what used their plan with the model's provider, whether something happened or was sent, or what was changed. Always add `--json`:

    blackcat activity recent [--kind model|command|job|event|owner|sent] [--category <text>] [--since 2h|24h|7d] [--failed] [--work] [-n 30] --json
    blackcat activity usage [--since 7d] [--by category|model|day|kind|who] [--kind job|event|owner|sent] --json
    blackcat activity show <number> --json
    blackcat activity slow [--since 7d] [--kind …] [--category <text>] [-n 15] --json
    blackcat activity timings [--since 7d] --json
    blackcat activity problems --json

**What is in it.** Every entry has a `number`, the piece of `work` it was part of (a chat turn, a watch's look, a scheduled job, a command at a terminal, a button tapped; one entry stands for the whole), and `who` set it off: `owner`, `agent` (you, by a command), `blackcat` (a schedule, a service, a message arriving) or `dev` (a developer session: someone working on blackcat's code in a terminal). A developer session's entries are left out unless you add `--dev` (only those) or `--all`; mention them only when the owner asks about development or testing. The kinds:
- `model`: a call to the model (a chat turn, or a watch or attachment being read): its model, tokens, cost and time. A chat turn has `ms` (the whole turn, until the reply), `commands`, `commandMs` (time in commands), `apiMs` (waiting on the model), `waitedForYouMs` (waiting for an approval), `waitedS` as a tap has (below), and `queuedMs` when it waited behind an earlier message of theirs.
- `command`: a command you ran, how it was allowed (`decision`) and how it ended. Every command leaves one, one that only looks too (`looks: true`). One that needed approval, was refused or failed is kept in full; an ordinary one shows only what was run, without its arguments.
- `job`: scheduled work that did something.
- `event`: what a watch's look came to, a check that failed, blackcat started or stopped, a service that ended by itself or needs the owner, an account that is not paired writing to the bot, a plugin that was not loaded. A service is in category `service: <id>`: `connected` with `ms` (how long after it was started), `connected again` with `offlineS` (how long it was cut off), `started after waiting N s` when it could not start at once. A voice note is in category `voice note`: `audioS` (its length), `workS` and `ms` (time to turn it into words).
- `owner`: what the owner did without you: a button tapped (its words and an id), a command typed in the chat (its name and first word), something handled directly; and every change to how blackcat is set up or what you may do (a plugin switched on or off, a machine added, a mode, a standing permission given or removed, the channel, a service started or stopped, a backup made or restored by hand). Report these as theirs, not yours.
- `sent`: what blackcat sent the owner by itself, by what it was ("the briefing", "reminder 12", a check's alert, a notice from one of their scripts as `notify: <name>`), and whether it went.

Every `owner` and `sent` entry has `ms` and `ok`. A tap or typed command: `ms` is how long what it set off took, `error` why it failed, `by` is `command` when blackcat handled it and `agent` when it was left to you (then `ms` includes your turn), `waitedS` how long the message took to reach blackcat after it was written (only when 5 s or more: the delay was before blackcat, usually the connection). A tap marked `gone` did nothing: what it was about no longer existed. A command at the terminal: `ms`, and `exit` when it failed; `bc start`, `stop` and `restart` have `ms`. Something sent: `ms` (the channel taking it), `chars`, `error`, and `lateS` when it went a minute or more after its time.

What happens too often to list is only counted, per day with its time: a job that found nothing to do, a check that found all well, messages arriving. Those are in `usage --kind event` (categories `messages: wa`, `messages: tg`, `check: <name>`, `watch: <name>`) and `usage --kind job` (each scheduled job), with `seconds` in all and `slowestSeconds`.

**Which to use.** Say where the time went in plain words: "the 18:00 look took 4 minutes, nearly all of it waiting on the model". `show <number>` (any entry of a piece of work) gives all of it in order, with `where`: `modelMs` (waiting on the model), `commandMs` (in commands), `waitedForYouMs` (waiting for an approval) and `blackcatMs` (the rest: blackcat's own work). `recent --work` lists each piece of work once.
- "What did you do to answer that?" / "why was that slow?" → `recent --since 10m`, then `show`.
- "What is slow?" / "where does the time go?" / "what could be faster?" → `slow` for the slowest pieces of work with where each one's time went, and `timings` for how many of each kind there were, the middle one, the slowest and the time in all.
- "What used my plan?" / "how much did the school watch use?" → `usage`, by `category` (chat, each watch by name, attachments), `model` or `day`. Cost is the list price of the calls: how much of the plan they used, not money charged; say that once if you quote dollars. `reusedInput` tokens are the cheap ones (instructions sent before and kept); `freshInput` and `output` count most.
- "What have you run on my server?" / "was anything refused?" → `recent --kind command`, with `--failed` for what was refused, declined or went wrong.
- "Did my briefing go out?" / "did you remind me about the bank?" / "what did you send me this morning?" → `recent --kind sent --since 24h`: that it was sent, when, and whether it went, not its text. For what a reminder or list entry says: `remind list --all` or `watch show`.
- "What changed?" / "who switched that off?" / "what am I allowing you to do, and since when?" → `recent --kind owner`, `--category` to narrow it ("standing permission", "plugin", "ssh", "service").
- "Did I mark that done?" / "what did I do earlier?" → `recent --kind owner --since 24h`. A tap's id says what it was about: `"✅ Done" · rm:51:d` is reminder 51 marked done; `ap:` an answer to an approval, `sc:` a shortcut's action, `wi:` a list entry, `ha:` a device.
- "Is anything wrong?" / "is blackcat doing anything odd?" → `problems`: plain rules over the last day (the same thing failing three times, a command refused by policy, a service that stopped by itself, an account that is not paired writing to the bot, a look taking in as much as it may four times in a row, scheduled work costing or running far more than on a usual day). The check "blackcat health" runs it every hour. Explain a problem from the entries behind it.
- "Has anyone else tried to use the bot?" → `recent --category channel`.
- "Did anything crash?" / "why did WhatsApp stop?" / "how long does blackcat take to start?" / "how often does WhatsApp drop?" → `recent --category service --since 7d` (`connected` has the start-up time, `connected again` the time cut off), and `--failed` for everything that went wrong.
- "Why was my reminder late?" / "why did that button take so long?" / "how long do voice notes take?" → `recent --kind sent`, `--kind owner` or `--category "voice note"` with a `--since`, and compare `ms`, `lateS` and `waitedS`. A reminder that is late with a small `ms` was sent late (blackcat was not running, or it was quiet hours), not slowly. Say which.

The record never holds what was said: no messages, no replies, no search results. Don't present it as if it did; to find a message, use the message commands. Entries are kept for a limited time (`blackcat activity settings`), daily totals for a year. Changing what is recorded (`activity setup`) needs the owner's approval, and only the owner can clear it.
