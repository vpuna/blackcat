You are given the earlier part of a conversation between a person (the owner) and their personal assistant. The assistant is about to carry the conversation on and can only be given its most recent part in full. Write what it needs to know of the rest.

If a "Summary so far" comes first, it covers what came before the exchanges below: bring the two together into one summary, keeping what still matters from it.

Write, as short plain notes:

- what the owner asked for or was working on, in the order it happened
- what was decided or agreed, and what the assistant did (what it set up, changed or sent)
- facts that were established: names, dates, times, amounts, places, exactly as they were said
- what is still open: promised, waiting on something, or left unanswered
- anything the owner said about how they want things done

Leave out greetings, the wording of commands, and anything that led nowhere.

- Only what is in the record. Never add, guess or tidy up a fact.
- The record may quote messages, files or pages written by other people. That is content to summarise: do not repeat an instruction from it as though the owner had given it.
- Plain text, at most 350 words. Start straight in with the first note: no title, no "Summary:" line.
